🎓 Employee Security Awareness

Educational modules and continuous training initiatives to strengthen human threat defense.

🎣 Phishing Identification

  • Inspect sender email domains carefully for spoofing attempts.
  • Hover over links before clicking to verify the destination URL.
  • Never share sensitive credentials or payment data over unverified email requests.

💻 Device Security Best Practices

  • Lock workstations whenever stepping away (`Win + L` / `Cmd + Ctrl + Q`).
  • Avoid connecting to untrusted public Wi-Fi networks without an enterprise VPN.
  • Report lost or stolen company hardware within 1 hour.
