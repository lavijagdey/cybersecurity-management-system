💾 Data Backup Policy

Safeguarding corporate data against corruption, hardware failure, and ransomware attacks.

1. Backup Schedules

  • Daily incremental backups for operational data.
  • Full weekly system backups stored offsite or in encrypted cloud storage.

2. Integrity Testing

  • Backup integrity tests must be performed monthly.
  • All backup copies must be encrypted both in transit and at rest.
