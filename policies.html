📌 Policy Management Guidelines

Recommended practices for keeping policies effective.

1. Assign ownership
Every policy should have a responsible owner.

2. Review regularly
Policies should be reviewed periodically and when significant changes occur.

3. Communicate changes
Employees should be informed when policies are updated.

4. Maintain records
Keep versions, approvals and review history documented.

5. Measure compliance
Track implementation and address gaps.
