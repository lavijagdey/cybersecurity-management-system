📖 Incident Response Policy

The following procedure guides employees and administrators during a security incident.

  1. Preparation: Train employees and maintain response procedures.
  2. Identification: Detect and document suspicious activity.
  3. Containment: Limit the incident's impact using approved procedures.
  4. Eradication: Remove the cause of the incident.
  5. Recovery: Restore affected services and verify their security.
  6. Lessons Learned: Document findings and improve security controls.
