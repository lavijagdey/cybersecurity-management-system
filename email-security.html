📧 Email Security Policy

Protecting company communications from phishing, malware, and social engineering.

1. Phishing & Malicious Attachments

  • Never open attachments from untrusted or unknown senders.
  • Verify sender email addresses carefully before clicking embedded links.
  • Report suspicious emails immediately to the IT security department.

2. Confidential Data Protection

  • Do not send sensitive personal or financial information via unencrypted email.
  • Auto-forwarding company emails to personal addresses is prohibited.
