🔐 Access Control Policy

Regulating access to critical systems and internal network resources.

1. Principle of Least Privilege

  • Users are granted access only to data and systems essential to their role.
  • Administrator access is strictly controlled and audited quarterly.

2. User Account Lifecycle

  • Access permissions are revoked immediately upon employee offboarding.
  • Shared or generic accounts are strictly prohibited.
