🔄 Recovery Testing

Why recovery tests are important

Backup Verification

Confirm backups exist, are accessible and can be restored. Verify the backup integrity and protection of stored data.

Restoration Exercise

Test restoration in a controlled environment and document actual recovery time against the RTO.

Business Continuity

Identify alternative processes, communication arrangements and critical dependencies during disruption.
